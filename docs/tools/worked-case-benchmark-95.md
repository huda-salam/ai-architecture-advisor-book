# Worked Case — “Our Benchmark Is 95%”

**Decision stage:** Selecting / Due diligence / Production approval

Do not ask whether 95% is “good.” Ask:

1. What task was measured?
2. What dataset and population were used?
3. What was the baseline?
4. How was the metric defined?
5. What uncertainty exists?
6. Which critical failures are hidden by the aggregate?
7. Does the test represent our workload?

Request the exact model/version, dataset, methodology, baseline, error slices, uncertainty, independent validation, and realistic workload evidence.

## Hidden Assumption

The central hidden assumption is that the benchmark is a valid proxy for the decision being made. That must be demonstrated rather than inferred from the headline score.

## Validation

Map benchmark tasks to the target workload, inspect failure slices, and where possible re-run representative evaluations rather than relying only on the aggregate score.

## Technical Position

A benchmark is evidence about a defined evaluation. It does not by itself establish suitability, production readiness, security, reliability, or business value.

**Advisor lesson:** Evaluate the measurement instrument before relying on the number.
